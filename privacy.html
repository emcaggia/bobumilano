Privacy policy

This covers bobumilano.com. It's short because the site collects very little.

Who is responsible

BOBU Milano runs this website and is the controller of the personal data it handles. Write to privacy@bobumilano.com.

What this site doesn't do

It has no accounts, forms, analytics, advertising or payments. It doesn't use your camera. The face scan isn't on this website.

What is handled, and why

Visit logs

Our host, GitHub Pages, logs your IP address for security when you visit. We don't receive these logs. GitHub decides how long it keeps them; see its privacy statement. Legal basis: our legitimate interest in keeping the site running and secure (GDPR Art. 6(1)(f)).

Your bag and saved items

These are stored in your browser and stay on your device. We never receive them. See the cookie policy.

Emails you send us

We use your address and message to reply, and our email provider stores them for us. We delete them once the matter is closed, unless the law requires us to keep them. Legal basis: our legitimate interest in answering you, or the steps you asked us to take (GDPR Art. 6(1)(b) and (f)).

People shown on the site

Members appear with their permission (GDPR Art. 6(1)(a)). They can withdraw it at any time, and we'll take their photo and profile down.

Who else sees it

We don't sell personal data or use it for advertising, and this site makes no automated decisions about you. Only the providers named above handle it. GitHub is based in the United States and certified under the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework. If any other provider is outside the EU, we rely on a lawful transfer mechanism such as standard contractual clauses.

Your rights

You can ask us to give you, correct, delete or restrict your data, to send it to you in a portable form, or to stop using it. Where we rely on your permission, you can withdraw it at any time. Write to privacy@bobumilano.com.

You can also complain to the data protection authority where you live. In Italy that is the Garante per la protezione dei dati personali.

The app

The BOBU app and its face scan aren't covered by this policy. They will have their own notice.

Changes

We'll post any change here and update the date above.
