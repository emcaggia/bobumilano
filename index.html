How it works

Three steps, one face.

  1. 1

    A quick look at your skin

    A 30-second scan in the app, in daylight. It reads the depth and undertone of your skin in seven places, and it stays on your phone.

  2. 2

    Tell us what you need

    Questions about how your skin behaves and what you love, and what you need from your makeup, in your own words.

  3. 3

    Mixed just for you

    Your formulas are mixed to order and arrive in a box with your name on it. Not quite right? Your first remix is free.

Yours

Your formula, your name.

Every formula is yours to name. Share your profile and others can add your formulas to their box. Foundation and concealer are re-matched to their skin; the colours stay yours.
