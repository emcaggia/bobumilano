What an ID does

One code, one formula.

  • 1

    It never changes

    The recipe behind an ID is fixed, so the same ID is the same formula in every box.

  • 2

    A new colour is a new ID

    Change the colour and you get a new formula with a new ID. The name you gave it stays.

  • 3

    The name is yours

    The code tells us what to mix. The name is what you call it, and you can change it whenever you like.

Wear someone else's

Love someone's lipstick? Buy their formula.

Every public profile is a small shop of the formulas someone has made.

Step 1

Find a formula

Browse members' profiles in the app. Each one shows their formulas and how many people wear them.

Step 2

Add it to your box

One tap and it joins your next box, with its own serial on the pack and their name beside it.

Step 3

Made for your skin

The colour and finish stay theirs. Your own scan fits the formula to your skin.

Share yours

Your formula, your name.

Your formulas become a small line of your own. Anyone in BOBU can add them to their box, and your profile shows how many people wear them.

Good to know

Do people see my scan?
No. The scan stays on your phone. Your profile shows your formulas.
Can I keep my formulas to myself?
Yes. Turn your profile off in the app whenever you like.
Where do I find my ID?
On every pack, and next to each formula in the app.
